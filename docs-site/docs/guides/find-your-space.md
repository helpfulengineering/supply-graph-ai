# Find your workshop on the map

Several thousand workshops are already in OHM, drawn from directories that
already existed. If you run or use a makerspace, fab lab, or university
workshop, there's a fair chance yours is one of them — without you having done
anything.

This costs nothing and needs no account.

## Look

Open the **Facilities** page. You'll see a map of every workshop OHM currently
knows about, clustered so that a few thousand pins stay legible, plus filters
down the side.

Narrow it down with:

- **Country**, **city**, and **region**
- **Process** — 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC machining, and so on
- **Source** — whether a record came from OHM directly or from Maps of Making
- **Access type** — how the space can be used

Filtering by country and then city is usually the fastest way to find a
particular place.

!!! note "Searching by name isn't available yet"

    You can't currently type a workshop's name and jump to it — you narrow by
    place and process instead. This is a known gap and a high priority to fix,
    because it's obviously the thing most people will try first.

## If you find yourself

Two things are probably true. Your entry exists, and it's thinner than it should
be.

Most records come from directories that describe **what a space is about** —
"digital fabrication", "electronics" — rather than what it can actually produce.
That's enough to place a pin on a map, and not enough for OHM to work out
whether you could build a particular design.

That gap is the single biggest limit on how useful OHM is right now. Closing it
for your own workshop is [described here](list-or-enrich-your-facility.md).

## If you don't find yourself

You can add your workshop directly. See
[list or enrich your facility](list-or-enrich-your-facility.md).

Worth knowing first: if your space publishes its details through Maps of Making,
that information lives **on your own website** and is read from there. Adding a
record in OHM is a separate thing from that, not a replacement for it, and it
doesn't move anything away from you.

## What you can't do

There's no "claim this workshop" button, and there deliberately never will be a
global one. In a network where every instance keeps its own records, a worldwide
ownership flag would be a fiction as soon as another instance disagreed.

What exists instead is attribution, domain verification, and vouching between
peers. See [what OHM is not](../about/what-ohm-is-not.md).
