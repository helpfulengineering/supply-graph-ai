# List or enrich your facility

Adding a workshop, or improving a record that's already there.

Before anything else, an honest statement of the deal. **Describing your
workshop properly takes your time, and the benefit to you is not immediate.**
There is no order flow yet. What you get in the near term is being findable by
people looking for what you can do, and being part of a network that exists
before it's urgently needed. It is our job to make that worth your time, not
yours to do us a favour.

## Why detail is the whole thing

Matching runs on capability detail. A record saying a space is interested in
"digital fabrication" places a pin on a map. A record saying it has a laser
cutter that takes 3 mm acrylic can answer an actual question.

Right now most records in OHM are the first kind. That's why a design needing
soldering currently matches almost nothing — not because the workshops can't
solder, but because nothing in their records says they can.

So enrichment isn't polish. It's the difference between OHM working and not.

## Adding or editing

Go to **Facilities** → **New facility**, or open an existing record and choose
**Edit**. The form covers:

- **Identity** — name, and how to get in touch
- **Location** — address, access, practical details like a loading dock
- **Equipment** — machines, by make and model
- **Processes** — what the space can actually do, chosen from a standard list so
  it means the same thing to everyone
- **Operations** — hours, capacity

You do not have to complete it in one go, and a partial record is genuinely
better than none. If you only do one thing, do **processes** — that is what
matching reads.

## Pick processes carefully

The process list is a shared vocabulary, which is what lets a design's
requirements line up with a workshop's capabilities across thousands of records.

Two things worth getting right:

**Claim what you can actually do**, not what your equipment could theoretically
support. A match that wastes somebody's afternoon is worse for you than no match.

**Do claim the unglamorous ones.** Soldering, assembly, drilling, finishing.
These are exactly the capabilities missing across the network today, and they are
frequently the ones that decide whether a design can be built at all.

## If your space is already on Maps of Making

Your record there is published by you, on your own site, and read from there.
Editing it in OHM does not change it, and does not take anything away from you.

If you'd rather improve things at the source, that's a better outcome than
duplicating effort here — the information reaches everything that reads it, not
just OHM.

## Then choose who sees it

A new record is **private** by default: it stays on this instance and is not
shared with anyone. Nothing is exported until you decide otherwise, and you can
share some parts while keeping others back.

See [who can see your data](who-can-see-your-data.md) — worth reading before you
enter an address.
